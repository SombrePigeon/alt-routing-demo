Necrotic Suffusion

Necromancer feature
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 15 feet

Starting at 1st level, you learn a simple incantation that allows you to wield negative energy. As an action, target one other creature within 15 feet of you. If that target is an undead under your control, they regain hit points equal to 1d8 plus your Charisma modifier. Otherwise, the target must succeed a Constitution saving throw or take necrotic damage equal to 1d8 plus your Charisma modifier. You may use this feature a number of times equal to your Charisma modifier (min. 1) and you regain all uses of this feature when you complete a long rest.

The damage and healing of this feature increases by 1d8 when you reach 5th level (2d8), 11th level (3d8), and 17th level (4d8).
