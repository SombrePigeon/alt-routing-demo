Soul Harvest

Necromancer feature
Casting Time: reaction

Starting at 3rd level, you learn to siphon the life from those unfortunate souls that you fell in battle. Whenever you or an undead under your control reduces a hostile creature that you can see to 0 hit points, you can use your reaction to catch the dying breath of that creature and use it to fortify your spirit. You gain temporary hit points equal to that creature's Constitution modifier plus your Necromancer Level (minimum of 1 temporary hit point).
